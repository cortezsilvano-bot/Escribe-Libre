# Moderation Operations

New direct listings move from draft to submitted and then to review. They cannot become publicly verified or published automatically. Reviewers inspect provider verification, address, source, prices/fees, image hashes, duplicate candidates, anomalies, attestations, reports, and history.

Approve only when the provider can advertise, facts are sufficiently complete, prohibited content is absent, and source/display rights are clear. Rejection, suspension, merge/unmerge, and manual verification require a written reason. The action, reason, previous/next state, actor, and request ID are immutable audit data.

Fraud and fair-housing scanners create explainable signals. Except for clear rate-limit abuse, automation does not ban accounts. Preserve evidence metadata without copying sensitive hosted identity documents into the database. Appeals use a new case linked to the original decision.

Direct listings expire after the configured period and must be renewed. Freshness and verification are separate public concepts.
