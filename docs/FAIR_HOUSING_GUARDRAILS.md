# Fair Housing Guardrails

- Ranking and targeting do not collect or infer race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, or other protected characteristics.
- Protected-class criteria and proxy intent are rejected in natural-language search. Ordinary keyword search remains available.
- No “safe,” “dangerous,” “good,” or “bad” neighborhood scores, demographic recommendations, or simplified crime grades exist.
- Accessibility features and voucher acceptance are factual, provider-reported fields; they are not renter profiles or ranking proxies.
- Sponsored inventory has explicit labeled slots and cannot replace organic results or use protected-class targeting.
- Ranking versions are logged and high-level factors are explainable: eligibility, geography, text, freshness, active verification, source trust, and completeness.
- A deterministic phrase scanner flags obvious exclusionary language for human review. It never silently rewrites the provider’s content.
- Jurisdiction rules are versioned data and every override requires an auditable reason.
- Manual testing includes searches phrased around protected classes and proxies to confirm rejection without hiding otherwise eligible listings.
